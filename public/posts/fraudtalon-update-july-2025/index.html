FraudTalon Updates: Explainable Analysis and Plans for QR Codes and URLs

July 16, 2025 | 2 minutes read

Tags: fraud, cybersecurity, project, update, fraudtalon

🚨 FraudTalon updates: now with explainable analysis

FraudTalon is evolving. The new update brings important features and sets the stage for the official launch, with a focus on education, accuracy, and protection against digital scams.

🧠 Explainable analysis: understand why it’s fraud

Now, regardless of the type of data analyzed — text, image, or email — the system presents a clear explanation of the reasons that led to the classification as possible fraud. This makes the experience far more educational, revealing the patterns used by scammers.

🎯 Goal: turn the analysis into an educational tool, not just a diagnostic one.
⏱️ Video, 02:52

🖼️ Support for images and .eml files already available

Support for uploading images and .eml files (complete emails) had already been present since the last version. The system is able to extract the content of those files for analysis, whether it’s a screenshot of a WhatsApp scam or a phishing email. What’s new is that it now also explains the reason for the suspicion, even in those cases.

📥 Example: got a suspicious invoice by email? Send the .eml or a photo and see the diagnosis with its justification.
⏱️ Video, 03:40

🔗 Coming soon: URL and QR Code analysis

Two features already in development:

  • URL validator: check whether a link points to a trustworthy site or one involved in fraud.
  • QR Code analyzer: decodes the QR and identifies risks, such as disguised phishing or suspicious redirects.

🚧 In implementation.
⏱️ Video, 04:10 and 04:22

🎯 Ever-increasing accuracy

The system already detects 99% of simulated cases. Even so, the focus now is refining the heuristic engines and the AI models even further, reducing false positives and improving assertiveness in ambiguous scenarios.

⚙️ More accuracy = less doubt when it’s time to act.
⏱️ Video, 04:39

🚀 Launch and early access

We’re still in the development phase, but we’ve already prepared a landing page for those interested. Anyone who wants to be notified with priority and get early access to the beta version will be able to register their email soon.

📬 The link will be shared in the coming days.
⏱️ Video, 06:06
